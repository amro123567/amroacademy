FAQ

Questions parents and students ask.

Can a strong SAT score help with scholarships?

It can. Scholarship policies differ by university, but College Board research and international guidance recognize SAT scores as one factor institutions may use in merit-based scholarship decisions. Always check the current policy of each university.

Do I need a high score before starting?

No. Preparation should begin from your actual starting point. The goal is to identify the highest-value improvements for your target.

Is tutoring available online or in person?

Both options are available. Students can study through online private tutoring, or request 1:1 in-home tutoring where a private tutor visits the student at home, subject to tutor availability and location.

Do you tutor international students outside Lebanon?

Yes. Online 1:1 SAT tutoring is available to students in any country. Sessions are scheduled around your time zone, and the free first diagnostic session is held online too — just choose your country in the form or message me on WhatsApp.

When should I start preparing?

Earlier is usually better because it gives you time to diagnose weaknesses, practice deliberately and take a realistic number of full-length tests before your target exam date.

What happens in the free first session?

It's a real 1-hour session held online — not a sales pitch. We run a diagnostic assessment to see the student's actual level in Math, Reading and Writing, walk through what the Digital SAT syllabus actually covers, and talk about university ambitions and longer-term goals. That conversation shapes everything that follows.

How much does tutoring cost?

Your first session is free, so you can experience the teaching style and see the value firsthand before any pricing conversation happens. After that, we'll discuss a plan and rate tailored to your goals, schedule and format (online or in-home) — no pressure, and no obligation to continue.

Do you guarantee a specific score?

No. No ethical tutor can guarantee a particular score. AmroAcademy focuses on structured preparation, measurable practice and helping each student maximize their own potential.
